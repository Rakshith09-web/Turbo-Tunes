Used only as the homepage hero's rotating background watermark — not part of the customizable
car catalog (see cars/seed_assets/README.md for those). hero-suv.glb is a re-export of the
original download, processed with glTF-Transform (https://gltf-transform.dev) to fit a
background element's weight budget:
- textures downscaled to 512px and re-encoded as WebP
- geometry simplified to ~35% of the original vertex count
- unused nodes/materials pruned, draw calls joined

Original download was ~50 MB (glTF + separate textures); this file is ~3.5 MB.
